# 60 g target: staged exploratory design, NOT a printing release

The current self-supporting spool is about 108 g under the user's real PETG profile. Independent deposited-path accounting attributes about 68.18 g to R<=44.5 mm central structures and 15.06 g to R>=95 mm rolling-ring/parking-slot area. Therefore 60 g cannot credibly be achieved by removing only the intermediate perforated skins, even before restoring the load-carrying spokes. These are slicing-path budgets, not a proof of the mathematical minimum of all possible redesigns.

Stage 1 retains the exact functional hub, all roof membranes, rolling ring and A parking slots. It replaces the full perforated annular skin with eight continuous native rectangular spokes and four connected thin double-C plaques. Plaques are decorative, not substitute load paths. This significantly changes lateral containment of the wound filament; a wound roll can bulge between spokes. Do not claim equivalent strength, spool containment or AMS function from unchanged dimensions.

Stage 2 can only be evaluated with new simulation and a clear tradeoff: reducing non-mating cylinder-wall thickness or support floors would change local buckling, torsion and shaft-support stiffness even if all nominal bore and lock surfaces remain. We will not silently remove the inner guide or modify the official lock to chase the numerical target.

Current printing model and its 3MF are unchanged. All work here is native parametric source, separate research outputs. Real slicing must use matched user settings; no density or infill changes may be hidden in a nominal 60 g claim.
