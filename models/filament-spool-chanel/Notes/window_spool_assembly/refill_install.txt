What is the Bambu Reusable Spool
We've designed the entire filament feeding system with a reusable spool, resulting in at least 20% less plastic waste in filament consumption. Our choice of packaging material is carefully considered to minimize the use of non-recyclable materials. There are two types of reusable spools: the high-temperature spool and the low-temperature spool.

Operation Procedure
¶
Step 1: Remove the Packaging from the New Filament
Prepare the new filament and the reusable spool. Then remove the packaging from the new filament.
📌 Note:
• If your filament has a clear protective strap,
do not peel it off yet.
• If you are using 250 g filament,
do not remove the filament's cardboard core.
¶
Step 2: Open the Reusable Spool
Turn the reusable spool counterclockwise to open it.
The wide half is on the left. The narrow half is on the right.
¶
Step 3: Align the Slots and Load the New Filament
📌 Note:
Do not remove the protective strap yet.
Find the clip on the wide half of the spool.
Align the notch on the cardboard core, press the filament firmly into place, and then attach the narrow half.

¶
Step 4: Secure the Filament End
If your filament has a protective strap
, first peel off the section of the strap closest to the filament's leading end. Then insert the filament end into the round hole on the spool to keep the filament from loosening. Finally, remove the rest of the protective strap.
If your filament does not have a protective strap
, remove the tape on the filament end, then insert the filament end into the round hole on the spool.
¶
Step 5: Press the Filament Firmly
After loading, check both sides of the filament. If there is a gap between the filament and the spool, press the edge of the filament coil by hand until the gap is filled. This prevents the filament from getting tangled.
¶
Step 6: Apply the Label
Finally, remove the old label from the spool. Then apply the label that came with the new filament.
