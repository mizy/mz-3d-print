This is a fully 3d printable strap for a Nintendo Switch Joy-Con that I
remixed after buying a used Nintendo Switch that came without any straps
for the Joy-Cons. It is a great solution if you've lost you straps or even if
you never had them like me.
The strap is modeled after the Nintendo version but, unlike the original,
this strap slides on from the bottom so that the button built into the JoyCon can lock the strap in place. While this lock is significantly better than
nothing it isn't perfect and can slip, especially after lots of wear and tear.
The buttons on the strap simply clip in with a little pressure and don't
come out. They also have a slightly pointed shape to make pressing the

button easy and to give it a snappy feel. Because the buttons don't have a
spring in them you may need to hold the strap with the buttons facing
down in order to slide the strap onto the Joy-Con. This is because the
buttons will block the rails from sliding when they are pressed in.
On the bottom end of the strap a peg as been modeled to attach any strap
you feel like adding that you might have laying around. You can also use
this without a strap if you just want an easier way to push the buttons. I
have also found that it is usually easier to use a bent paper clip to pull the
added strap around the peg.
